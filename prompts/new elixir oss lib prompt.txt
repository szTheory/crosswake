tldr; the top comment is correct. No production apps.

tbh the direction the project is going in the moment means LiveView Native as a concept is likely dead in the water. The cost of having to constantly fix breaking changes in each minor version bump of LiveView is more than I'm willing to continue to absorb. A complete and total lack of cooperation from the LiveView team as well has been incredibly disheartening. They'll probably claim they were open to help but beyond three specific changes we got into Elixir or LiveView (multi-char sigils, render_with, and a change to the template engine) they refused to budge on the largest problem which is that LiveView is hard coded for HTML.

What we're working towards is a general solution for all JavaScript frameworks to buld native apps. If you can build a HTML web app you can build a native app. Unfortunately this means that LiveView will be left behind because it decided it wanted to hold its ground. It could have been the only framework with a real server-driven UI solution for native application development but egos gotta ego I guess.

This does put me out on the limb for not delivering on LVN. But I'm not willing to put myself out of business and drive myself into the looney bin building on top of that project any longer. I highly suggest others don't either. It's not built for extensibility which is a large reason why it has performed so poorly with adoption. Devs gravitate towards solutions that allow them to work with how they think. If you're forced into another lane then you're just going to look elsewhere.
