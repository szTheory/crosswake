# v6.0 Closeout Gate: Adoption Evidence Demo App (Flashcard Cohort)

## Milestone Objective
The goal of Milestone v6.0 was to provide concrete adoption evidence and demonstrate the `Crosswake.Offline` island philosophy. This was achieved by building a Language Learning / Flashcard app, intentionally chosen to rigorously stress-test offline scenarios and eventual consistency.

## Gate Results

### 1. Stability of Offline Workflows
- **Validation**: Playwright E2E network-toggling tests have been successfully implemented and integrated into the CI/CD pipeline (`.github/workflows/offline-sync-e2e-gate.yml`).
- **Result**: **PASS**. The E2E tests prove the offline study loop functions as intended, allowing actions to be locally recorded and subsequently synchronized to Ecto state upon reconnection.

### 2. Adoption Guidance
- **Validation**: An adoption guide (`guides/adoption.md`) was drafted to detail the demo app's architecture and the integration of the offline-sync architecture.
- **Result**: **PASS**. Users are now equipped with clear steps on integrating the standalone Crosswake shell and correctly handling eventual consistency.

### 3. Security and Mitigation (T-90-01)
- **Validation**: Post-reconnection syncing properly validates Ecto state. The E2E tests specifically act as a mitigation validation for potential tampering by explicitly asserting valid Ecto states post-reconnect.
- **Result**: **PASS**.

## Summary
The v6.0 Milestone "Adoption Evidence Demo App (Flashcard Cohort)" has successfully met all its objectives. The standalone SPM/Maven dependencies pattern allows users to completely sidestep the "eject trap". This milestone provides solid evidence and rigorous testing to prove that Crosswake can handle demanding offline workflows natively and synchronize them reliably.

The Closeout Gate is **APPROVED**.
