# Personas and jobs

Synthetic and generic; never modeled on a real customer. Requirements carry a tag: P1-P7, J1-J5, a combination such as P1/J2, or M (maintainer-infra).

## Personas

- P1: solo or tiny-team Phoenix founder with an offline-capable B2C product (primary).
- P2: small B2C or SaaS startup team.
- P3: agency shipping Phoenix apps (weak fit).
- P4: enterprise SSO/MDM team (out of scope; documented honestly).
- P5: skeptical mobile engineer (veto holder).
- P6: security or privacy officer (gatekeeper).
- P7: cross-platform B2C team where Android is required.

## Jobs

- J1: first iPhone route.
- J2: offline island that survives logout and account switch.
- J3: know why it failed.
- J4: upgrade without surprise.
- J5: prove it to a skeptic.

## Maintainer

- M: maintainer infrastructure work that serves no single persona directly (CI, release, planning hygiene).
