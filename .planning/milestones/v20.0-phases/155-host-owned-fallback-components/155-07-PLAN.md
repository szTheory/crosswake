<objective>
Land PROOF-01: a merge-blocking browser route-tour proving the surface renders, fails closed
with an explicit denial rather than a silent success, and never resolves to silence — plus the
record corrections the proof's own wording depends on.

Three routes because there are three conditions. A1 proves the surface renders on a real
product route. A2 proves a shell-side denial renders and the mutation does not proceed. A3
proves no failure path resolves to silence, honestly approximated by enumerating the closed
denial vocabulary and requiring exercised union explicitly-excluded to equal the full set.

The success criterion as written in ROADMAP.md is wrong and this plan corrects it. "Undeclared"
is three distinct moments, not one: an outbound preflight raise that renders nothing, a
shell-side rejection that renders a denial, and a vocabulary miss that also raises. Only the
middle one is browser-observable. The corrected wording says so, and labels the split hybrid.

Purpose: make the phase's central claim falsifiable, and make what it does NOT claim equally
explicit.
Output: one ~200-line spec riding an existing required lane, one new environment-gated route,
one mutation control, a failure-artifact upload, and four record corrections.
</objective>
