# Milestone v20.0: Native Controls Pack 1

**Status:** STOPPED / PARTIAL on 2026-07-30  
**Phases:** 153-157  
**Reason:** Replaced by v21 First B2C Adopter Readiness. This is not a shipped-milestone claim and
no v20.0 completion tag should be created.

## Delivered and retained

- Phase 153: iOS mirror unblock.
- Phase 153.1: CI gate integrity and runner-cost work.
- Phase 154: typed control-contract seam, fail-closed denial, structural catalog guard, and haptics
  migration.
- Phase 155: host-owned generated fallback components.

These outputs remain available substrate. Their presence does not justify continuing the original
native-control catalog.

## Stopped

- Phase 156 native menu/action-button implementation.
- Phase 157 haptics/share hardening and proof-promotion bundle.
- Android native-control parity and device proof associated with those phases.

Phase 156 research, UI specification, source audit, validation, and plans are retained as abandoned
planning evidence. They are not implementation evidence.

## Why the milestone stopped

The first real adopter needs a host-reusable proof lane, privacy-safe replay, verified offline
pronunciation media, auth continuity, server-side disablement, and physical-iPhone evidence. A
new native menu does not unblock that path. Continuing v20 would optimize the framework surface
while delaying its first real use.

## Verification truth

The v20 milestone audit reported gaps and only Phase 153 had the audit's formal verification
posture at the time of closeout. Phase 154 and Phase 155 have implementation summaries and
verification artifacts, but v20 is intentionally not represented as fully verified or shipped.
Phases 156 and 157 are incomplete.

## Reversal condition

Resume the stopped native-control work only after the physical-iPhone adopter proof passes and an
active adopter route demonstrates that the control removes a real product blocker.
