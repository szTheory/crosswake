<threat_model>

| T-56-01 | Spoofing | `StepUpIntentLocator` | Client locator smuggles identity, session, provider, or authority claims. | Reject forbidden locator fields and keep locator contents low-sensitivity. |
| T-56-02 | Tampering | Step-up lifecycle contract | Callers construct unsupported lifecycle states that bypass replay/cancel/expiry proof. | Closed lifecycle vocabulary and constructor validation. |
| T-56-03 | Information Disclosure | Denial details | Shell-safe denial details leak raw tokens, refs, identifiers, or provider material. | Extend only allowlisted safe detail keys and test unsafe drops. |
| T-56-04 | Elevation of Privilege | Completion contract | Completion succeeds without backend `SessionAuthorityLane`. | Require `%SessionAuthorityLane{}` plus host-owned renewal instructions. |
</threat_model>
