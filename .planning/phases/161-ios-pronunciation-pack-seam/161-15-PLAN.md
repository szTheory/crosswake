<objective>
Close the remaining PACK-03 blocker with a durable, restart-recoverable host publication transaction.

Purpose: Make last-known-good preservation and inventory publication survive process kill, crash, or shutdown—not only Swift throws—without widening the provider contract or retaining sensitive data.
Output: A host-private fsynced replacement journal, one construction-time startup-recovery barrier, side-effect-free status behavior, and deterministic restart-state XCTest coverage.
</objective>

<gap_root_cause>
Plan 161-12 made replacement exception-safe, but `publicationState`, `defer`, and `catch` exist only in process memory. Termination can occur after the old artifact is retained, after the new artifact is promoted, or after inventory is committed but before cleanup. `status(for:)` currently reads only the destination and `inventory.json`, so no later provider instance can distinguish or repair those states. This leaves D-04/D-06/D-08/D-17 and PACK-03 unproved under ordinary process interruption.
</gap_root_cause>
