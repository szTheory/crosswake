## Key Decisions

- The two `examples/` `crosswake_manifest.json` files are hand-maintained tracked fixtures, not live generator output. The `mix crosswake.gen.shell` task writes through `ensure_file/2` (scaffold-once semantics) into a different path (`native/ios/crosswake_shell` / `native/android/crosswake_shell`). It cannot and does not overwrite committed `examples/` manifests. The fix was therefore a direct in-place targeted edit of the one drifted field.
- Drift-coverage of `crosswake_manifest.json` via generate-and-diff guard is deferred to Phase 122. Adding manifest emission to `contract.gen` would create split ownership of one file across two generators. Phase 122 will decide: (a) a parity assertion that the committed manifests' bridge axis equals `Contract.version()`, or (b) giving `gen.shell` authority to re-emit the committed `examples/` manifests for diffing. This handoff note surfaces the item rather than dropping it.

## Phase 122 Handoff Note

`crosswake_manifest.json` drift-coverage is a Phase 122 guard-scoping decision. The two options are documented in `121-04-PLAN.md`'s `<phase_122_handoff>` section. Do not fold `crosswake_manifest.json` emission into `contract.gen` — that would split ownership with `gen.shell`/`Fixtures` over a single file.
