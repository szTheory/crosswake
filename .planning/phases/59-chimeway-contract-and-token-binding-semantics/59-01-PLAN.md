# Plan 59-01: Chimeway Companion Contract And Lifecycle Vocabulary

<objective>
Define the in-tree Chimeway companion entrypoint and the pure-Elixir token evidence, token binding, provider feedback, binding event, and binding result contracts that Phase 60 will persist and mutate without revisiting public vocabulary.
</objective>

<context>
Phase 59 is contract-only. Do not add Ecto schemas, backend transactions, provider credentials, notification-open routing, RouteGate activation sources, doctor/support/operator rollout, or delivery guarantees here. Keep the public contract compact, typed, and provider-neutral.
