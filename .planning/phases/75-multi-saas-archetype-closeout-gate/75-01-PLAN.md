Verify all archetype proofs are CI-hermetic and requirements are met for v4.1.

## Decisions Made (Discuss Phase)
1. **Closeout Verifier Milestone Detection**: `mix closeout.verify` will be updated to auto-detect the active milestone from `.planning/STATE.md` (e.g., `v4.1`) instead of hardcoding `v4.0`.
