## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-159-10 | Repudiation | native prerequisite outcomes | high | mitigate | Closed outcomes, stable prerequisite IDs, no skip APIs, non-empty tests, and negative assertions against `.passed`. |
| T-159-11 | Elevation of Privilege | test-only driver | high | mitigate | Opaque refs and outcomes only; backend endpoint remains authoritative and driver has no credentials or action authority. |

| T-159-SC | Tampering | native tooling/templates | low | accept | No package is installed; static repository templates and Apple platform frameworks are the only native inputs. |
