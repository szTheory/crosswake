## Shared Patterns

### Stable non-echoing denial

**Sources:** `physical_iphone_preflight.ex`, lines 57-68; `route_inventory.ex`, lines 446-453.

Use rule IDs plus a generic remediation. Never serialize rejected handoff values, scope refs, account data, media facts, or payloads in the response, logs, report, evidence, or guide.

### Fresh authority at side-effect boundaries

**Sources:** `PhysicalIphonePreflight.check/1` lines 45-66; `NavigationCoordinator.apply/1` lines 73-98; `PackStore.reconcile/3` lines 172-195.

Revalidate handoff/topology before physical operations, reauthorize every native transition, and reconcile provider status before route availability. Cached successful state is not authority.

### Exact tests and transaction cleanup

**Sources:** Mix task lines 126-143 and 216-243; evidence decoder lines 594-645.

Tests must reject omitted/reordered/wrong-owner report assertions and unknown artifact paths. Device/backend cases may reset only between isolated negative cases; happy-path offline save → relaunch → same-ID retry must be uninterrupted.
