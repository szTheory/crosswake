## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-85-01 | Tampering | EventLog Schema | mitigate | Use database-level unique constraints (`unique_constraint(:idempotency_key)`) to prevent duplicate replays from tampering with authoritative state. |
| T-85-02 | Denial of Service | SyncController | mitigate | Use `Ecto.Multi.insert_all` with `on_conflict: :nothing` to handle batch insertions atomically and efficiently without N+1 query race conditions. |
</threat_model>
