extraction cannot repeat the coupling footguns the family already hit. Implements FAMILY-02 per
D-10, D-11, D-12, D-13, D-14.

even on a match — it can never fail CI), widens the guard scope + adds a module-attribute pass,
documents the observer variant and the flagged-legit-core-file case, and adds inline failure
callouts + an updated proven-on footer.

can't fail is theater. Step 0 makes prerequisite decoupling an active numbered step instead of
buried prose, so an engineer can't start "move source" before core is decoupled and then hit an
undiagnosable compile failure.
