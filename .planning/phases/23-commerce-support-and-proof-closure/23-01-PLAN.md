<threat_model>
## Security Gate

- standard: ASVS L1
- block_on: high

## Trust Boundaries

- Doctor output crosses from internal diagnostics to operator/CI consumption as support truth.
- Proof_class labels become gate criteria; misclassification could suppress blocking failures or over-block advisory checks.
- Stale-snapshot detection uses freshness metadata that could be absent or default; fail-closed behavior prevents silent access grants.

## STRIDE Threat Register

| threat_id | category | component | disposition | mitigation_plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P23-01-T01 | tampering | proof_class classification | mitigated | Derive proof_class from canonical support matrix entries, not per-finding heuristics; lock expected values in tests. |
| P23-01-T02 | elevation_of_privilege | Stale snapshot treated as informational | mitigated | Enforce fail-closed: stale or unknown freshness always emits merge-blocking finding severity. |
| P23-01-T03 | information_disclosure | Commerce summary exposes internal route details | mitigated | Summary uses corridor roles and canonical codes only; no route paths or provider identifiers in summary output. |
| P23-01-T04 | denial_of_service | False-positive rebuild requirements block development | mitigated | Rebuild requirement derived from support matrix metadata (not route heuristics); advisory provider checks do not block merge. |
</threat_model>
