<objective>
Close the phase with the documentation pass that keeps its claims honest, then MECHANIZE the six
judgements that were originally staged as a human checkpoint on the desktop-browser evidence panel,
the doctor advisories, and the catalog guard's failure message.

Phase 154 ships with ZERO human verification and ZERO UAT. That is the shift-left pattern this
project established with PROOF-03 / Phase 135, which converted its "human sign-off" items into a
hermetic proof lane on existing merge-blocking workflows rather than leaving a standing ask. The
goal is 0 recurring intervention: only genuine, irreversible go-decisions stay human, and "does this
panel read right?" is not one — it decomposes into copy identity, DOM order, ARIA semantics, a
computed contrast ratio, and node identity across a mutation, all of which are decidable.

What is NOT claimed: several of the resulting checks are proxies for the judgement they replace, and
each says so in its own docblock rather than borrowing the authority of the thing it stands in for.

D-77 named scope honesty as this phase's biggest risk. The corresponding documentation risk is
claim inflation: three of this phase's requirements have a strong form and a weaker true form, and
the guides are where the difference either gets stated or gets lost. CTRL-04 closes the mechanical
plugin-catalog road completely but does not stop a maintainer adding forty controls one string at a
time (D-45). CTRL-05's enforce-keys change is the only part that earns "structurally impossible";
everything else is CI-caught (D-52). CTRL-01's reply half is satisfied on Android natively, on every
platform by server-synthesized denials, and on iOS only in-repo until the Phase 156 release (D-03).

Purpose: this project's brand is compatibility honesty. A guide that overclaims on the milestone's
headline seam costs more than the feature earns.

Output: five guides that state the true form of each claim, and eight merge-blocking automated
checks (A–H) replacing the phase's last human gate — every one of them demonstrated capable of
failing, and every proxy labelled as one.
</objective>
