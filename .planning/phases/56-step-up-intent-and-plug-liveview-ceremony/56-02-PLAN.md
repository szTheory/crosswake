<threat_model>
| ID | STRIDE | Asset | Risk | Mitigation |
|----|--------|-------|------|------------|
| T-56-04 | Replay | Step-up intent row | A locator is consumed twice or after cancel/revoke/expiry. | Conditional Ecto consume with state/timestamp predicates and affected-row assertion. |
| T-56-05 | Tampering | Return target | Caller uses raw `return_to` or mismatched route to redirect after challenge. | Store and validate manifest-known route IDs only; reject raw return URLs. |
| T-56-06 | Elevation of Privilege | Session authority projection | Challenge evidence directly grants route access. | Build and validate a refreshed `SessionAuthorityLane`, then re-run RouteGate on the return route. |
| T-56-07 | Session Fixation | Phoenix session | Privilege/freshness change keeps stale session/CSRF posture. | Return host instructions to renew session, rotate CSRF posture, delete transient keys, and invalidate LiveView sockets. |
</threat_model>
