<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Device/storefront evidence -> backend reconciliation contract | Untrusted purchase or restore callbacks may be duplicated, stale, or malicious and must be normalized before any entitlement projection changes. |
| Backend reconciliation contract -> entitlement snapshot readers | Phoenix routes and native consumers trust the projected snapshot, so stale or pending states must stay explicit. |
| Public commerce guide -> adopters | Guide wording determines whether teams mistakenly grant access from client success or claim unsupported offline authority. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-13-05 | Tampering | `lib/crosswake/commerce/reconciliation.ex` | mitigate | Encode evidence submission and authority projection as separate typed stages so device-side callbacks cannot directly transition access state in core, per D-15 and D-17. |
| T-13-06 | Repudiation | `guides/commerce.md`, `test/crosswake/guides/commerce_test.exs` | mitigate | Lock the canonical flow, authority/evidence wording, and offline non-goals in a guide-contract test so support claims cannot later deny backend-authority requirements were documented. |
| T-13-07 | Denial of Service | `lib/crosswake/commerce/reconciliation.ex` | mitigate | Model provider-aware idempotency keys and explicit reconciliation outcomes per D-16 so duplicate evidence and webhook retries can be deduplicated by hosts rather than multiplying projection work. |
| T-13-08 | Elevation of Privilege | `lib/crosswake/commerce/contracts.ex`, `guides/commerce.md` | mitigate | Keep `pending_purchase`, `pending_restore`, and `awaiting_verification` as reconciliation states only, not access grants, so provisional client states cannot elevate entitlement authority. |
</threat_model>
