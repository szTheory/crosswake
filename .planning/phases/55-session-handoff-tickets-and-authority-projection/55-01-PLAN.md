## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| signed envelope claims -> backend handoff contract | Untrusted client-presented ticket data crosses into backend-owned contract validation. |
| internal denial facts -> shell-safe denial details | Rich backend auth facts cross into user-visible or support-visible denial payloads. |

| T-55-02 | Spoofing | `Crosswake.Companions.Sigra.Handoff.HandoffEnvelope` | mitigate | Reject authority-bearing or identity-bearing claims and keep envelope contents bounded low-sensitivity locator/correlation data per D-02/D-03. |
