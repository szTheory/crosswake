<severity_decision>
RESEARCH Pitfall 1 / Open Question 1 is resolved here: D-03 specifies `:info` severity intent,
but `Crosswake.Doctor.Check.severity()` is `:error | :warning | :advisory` and the formatter's
`severity_order/1` (lib/crosswake/doctor/formatter.ex lines 389-391) only handles those three
atoms — an `:info` atom would raise FunctionClauseError. Use `:advisory` for per-route gating
info findings. `:advisory` carries the same "informational, not a problem" semantics D-03
describes and requires no formatter changes. Do NOT add `:info` to the severity type.
</severity_decision>
