| T-132-01 | Spoofing | slopsquat/typosquat of the `rindle` Hex engine in the clean-room | medium | mitigate | `rindle` confirmed on Hex, same author `szTheory`, versions 0.1.4–0.1.10 + 0.3.0; engine cap `~> 0.1` resolves a known `0.1.10`, no wildcard could pull a hijacked package (RESEARCH.md §Security Domain) |
