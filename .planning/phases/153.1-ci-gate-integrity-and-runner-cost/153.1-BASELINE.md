# Phase 153.1 — Baseline Measurement

**Measured:** 2026-07-28
**Reference push:** `3ac6a54d` (merge of #89 into `main`)
**Why this push:** a real merge commit on `main` with the full workflow set triggered — not a
synthetic run, and not a PR (PR runs see different cache and concurrency behavior).

All numbers below are measured. Where a number could not be measured it says so; nothing here is an
estimate presented as a measurement.

---

## Headline: the cost is queue, not compute

Per-**job** timings from the Actions jobs API, all 77 jobs on the reference push:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Jobs on one push | **77** |
| Total **queued** (`created_at` → `started_at`) | **9,454 s** (2.63 h) |
| Total **executed** (`started_at` → `completed_at`) | **4,872 s** (1.35 h) |
| Total | **14,326 s** (3.98 h) |
| **Queue share of total** | **66.0 %** |

Two thirds of what a push costs in wall-clock is jobs sitting in line.

## The queue is almost entirely macOS

| Runner class | Jobs | Total queue | Total exec | **Mean queue/job** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `macos-15` / `macos-latest` | 19 | **9,305 s** | 2,886 s | **490 s** |
| `ubuntu-latest` | 58 | 149 s | 1,986 s | **3 s** |

- macOS is **24.7 % of jobs** but **98.4 % of all queue time**.
- Mean queue per job is **163× higher** on macOS than on ubuntu.

This confirms the SEED-007 claim (which cited a 75–250× range) with in-repo measurement, and it is
the entire thesis of `RUNNER-*`: these jobs are not slow, they are *waiting*.

## What could not be measured

- **Cache hit rate.** There is nothing to measure yet — only 6 of 39 workflows cache anything and
  none cache `deps/`. Plan 03 Task 4 captures this after the first merge to `main`, since caches are
  branch-scoped and only `main` writes entries other branches can read.
- **Billed runner-minutes.** The measurement above is wall-clock. Actual billing applies the 10×
  macOS multiplier to *execution* time only, not queue time — so the billing picture and the
  wall-clock picture are different problems. This phase targets wall-clock (developer latency);
  billing improves as a side effect of the same moves.

## How to re-derive

The per-job timing table comes from iterating `gh run list --commit <sha>` and then
`gh api repos/szTheory/crosswake/actions/runs/<id>/jobs`, differencing `created_at` → `started_at`
(queue) and `started_at` → `completed_at` (exec), grouping by the job's `labels`. Plan 03 Task 5
re-runs the identical procedure against the post-change `main` to produce `153.1-RESULTS.md`.
