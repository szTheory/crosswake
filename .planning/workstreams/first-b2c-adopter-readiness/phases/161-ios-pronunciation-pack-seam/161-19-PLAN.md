<objective>
Repair the crash-recovery invariant that confuses stale inventory with retained pronunciation bytes.

Purpose: Keep the host foreground installer usable after the one reachable promotion-pending interruption without weakening fail-closed recovery or last-known-good preservation.
Output: A production provider invariant repair and a direct executable stale-inventory/no-artifact relaunch regression.
</objective>

<gap_root_cause>
`install` currently loads `priorRecord` independently from `hadPriorArtifact`. With stale inventory and no destination, it can fsync a `.promotionPending` journal containing a prior record even though no retained file was created. Startup recovery then requires the nonexistent retained file, throws, and the construction-time recovery task memoizes that failure for every public operation.
</gap_root_cause>
