**Rows are occurrences, not files.** Several files carry multiple, differently-classified
occurrences; ROADMAP criterion #5 is checked at the occurrence level. Every row carries: `File:Line`
(post-repair line number, re-grepped — not the research-era number), the verbatim literal context,
the classification, the evidence for that classification, and the disposition (what this phase did to
it, or why it was left).

**Classification vocabulary — six values, not four.** The four from the requirement, plus two the
verified findings forced:
  - `live gate` — controls whether a job or function proceeds
  - `fixture` — a value bound to one specific historical event, correctly permanent
  - `docstring` — prose or comment describing a past state
  - `display string` — human-readable label or log text that gates nothing
  - `orphaned validator (no production caller; test-only)` — `coordinate.ex`'s occurrences, per D-171-C.
    Do NOT flatten these into `live gate`: the verified grep found no production caller, and recording
    them as live gates would misstate the finding. Record the grep command and its result in the row's
    evidence cell.
  - `live gate, premise inverted` — `ios-mirror-backfill.yml`'s attestation negative controls, per
    D-171-D: they execute and can fail, but their premise (the artifact is not yet public) has since
    inverted, so the path was unsatisfiable at any version before this phase.

Carry forward, unchanged, the two classification distinctions the research flags as easy to get wrong:
  - `ios_mirror.sh`'s two version constants are NOT the same weld. The baseline constant is a `fixture`
    (a permanently-historical pre-candidate tag); the candidate constant was a `live gate`. Classify by
    function, from the mode dispatch — not by the shape of the grep hit (171-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 2).
  - `mirror.ex`'s `@baseline_version` is likewise a `fixture` and `@candidate_version` was a `live gate`.

Also record `mix.exs`'s own `@version` as explicitly OUT of scope with its reason: it is the
authoritative source `approved-release-guard` reads FROM, not a copy of that fact elsewhere.

Add a closing section stating the acceptance fact in measured form: the count of rows classified
`live gate` that still reference a bare version literal. That number must be `0`, and it must be a
counted number derived from the table, not a claim.

Per DOC-05, the inventory's own prose says "release manifest" when it means
`.release-please-manifest.json`, and qualifies the other two ("runtime manifest",
"approved-manifest matrix") wherever they appear.
