## STRIDE Threat Register (ASVS L1; block on high)

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-192-06 | Spoofing | a same-named check from another app or an older head counted as the required check | high | mitigate | Select by name, app id and head_sha, then highest id; controls C02-C05. |
